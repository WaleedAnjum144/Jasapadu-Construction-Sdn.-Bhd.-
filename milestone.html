OUR HISTORY

A Journey Built on Experience & Excellence

Since its incorporation in 2002, Jasapadu Construction Development Sdn. Bhd. has continued to grow through dedication, technical expertise and a commitment to quality. From early building developments to infrastructure and specialized construction projects, each milestone represents another step in our journey towards delivering reliable construction solutions.

EST. 2002
01
THE BEGINNING

2002

Company Established

Jasapadu Construction Development Sdn. Bhd. was officially incorporated on 27 November 2002, marking the beginning of its journey in the construction industry.

02
EARLY PROJECT EXPERIENCE

2003

Scania Factory

Undertook the Scania Factory project in Shah Alam, gaining valuable experience in industrial construction and floor surface preparation.

03
PORTFOLIO EXPANSION

2004

Expanding Project Portfolio

Expanded project experience through the Service Apartment development in Pekan Serdang and SIRIM RAMET project in Rasa, Hulu Selangor.

04
BUILDING DEVELOPMENT

2006 – 2007

Villa Maarof Development

Continued developing its construction capabilities through the Villa Maarof project in Sungai Penchala, Kuala Lumpur.

05
INDUSTRY RECOGNITION

2007

CIDB Registration

Registered with the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) on 5 April 2007, establishing its registered contractor credentials with Grade G7 qualifications.

06
PROJECT DIVERSIFICATION

2007 – 2010

Residential & Infrastructure Projects

Delivered a range of developments, including Terrace and Semi-Detached Housing in Shah Alam and the Marine Police Base project in Lahad Datu.

07
MAJOR DEVELOPMENT

2015 – 2018

Yayasan Melaka Office

Undertook the Yayasan Melaka Office design and build project in Melaka, further strengthening its building development experience.

08
PROFESSIONAL RECOGNITION

2025

Strengthening Our Credentials

Achieved a 3-Star CIDB SCORE rating for 2025 and maintained Grade G7 registration. The company also registered with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia, expanding its professional credentials and capabilities.
