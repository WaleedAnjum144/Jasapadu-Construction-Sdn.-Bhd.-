Directors Introduction

Muhammad Shariffudin

Director

Muhammad Shariffudin has served as a Director of Jasapadu Construction Development Sdn. Bhd. since November 2024 and is a shareholder of the company. He is committed to building long-term relationships with clients based on integrity, performance, value and client satisfaction, and to protecting the company’s reputation for excellent customer service.

As an authorised officer under the company’s CIDB and Ministry of Finance registrations, he helps keep the company’s Grade G7 standing and its government procurement credentials in good order.

Muhammad Adam bin Muhammad Siddiq Ravishanker

Director

Muhammad Adam joined the board in May 2025. He works closely with the building team, owners, architects and other parties on each project, so that works are completed in a timely fashion and to the standard clients expect.

He is an authorised officer under the company’s CIDB Grade G7 registration and supports the people side of the business, helping the company meet its clients’ changing needs with qualified staff.

Muhammad Siddiq Ravishanker

General Manager

Muhammad Siddiq Ravishanker leads day-to-day operations as General Manager, overseeing the project, quantity surveying and purchasing, accounts, and administration teams. He emphasises structured organisation and time management, which helps projects finish on schedule and supports better pricing on later projects.

He insists on responsible subcontractors who are qualified for each specific project, and keeps open dialogue with past, current and potential clients.
