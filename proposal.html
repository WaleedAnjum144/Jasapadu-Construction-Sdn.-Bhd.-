100 MW Kuantan
Data Center Campus

A phased hyperscale and wholesale colocation campus in the Kuantan district, Pahang. Jasapadu acts as development sponsor and local construction delivery lead, working with an experienced data center operator and specialist engineers.

Develop in stages, led by utility capacity

A 100 MW IT campus is technically plausible in the Kuantan region, but no plot has been assessed and no power or water has been reserved. This is a prospect for investigation, not a statement that capacity already exists.

Jasapadu would form a project SPV to hold the land, utility agreements, customer leases and financing, and would not commit to a full 100 MW construction award until power, water, customers and funding are secured.

Proceed only if utility studies confirm a supply date and cost, authorities accept the development, a qualified operator is appointed and a tenant commits to the first phase.

What the proposal covers

Six workstreams that decide whether the campus can be built.

Land and site

80-120 acres searched with a 100-acre reference masterplan. Land is secured through a conditional option tied to due diligence.

Electricity

About 135 MW of facility demand at PUE 1.35, with a staged high-voltage supply application to TNB.

Water

Target site WUE of 0.30-0.80 L/kWh, starting the PAIP allocation request at 2.5 MLD average capacity.

Technical design

Four independent 25 MW blocks with concurrently maintainable infrastructure and high-density readiness.

Approvals

Early engagement with MIDA, TNB, PAIP, DOE, BOMBA and local authorities before any land is bought.

Commercial model

Wholesale capacity leased to a creditworthy anchor tenant, with 15-20 MW pre-leased for Phase 1.

Programme from project start

Overlapping planning windows, not booked completion dates. Grid reinforcement can move the whole schedule.

  1. Months 0-6 Feasibility Site shortlist, operator selection, TNB and PAIP studies.
  2. Months 4-12 Land and approvals Conditional land control, submissions, concept design.
  3. Months 10-20 Design and finance Detailed design, finance close, long-lead equipment orders.
  4. Months 16-34 Construction Enabling works, first block, substation delivery.
  5. Months 30-42 Phase 1 opens Integrated testing, operator training, customer acceptance.
  6. Months 36-84 Phases 2-4 Built in step with demand, approvals and utility expansion.
